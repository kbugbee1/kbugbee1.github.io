# About

I work in scientific informatics and data stewardship, with an interest in preserving scientific knowledge and making research outputs accessible, understandable, and reusable.

This independent personal website is a place to share writing, talks, and ideas about the people, practices, and technologies behind scientific discovery.

## Areas of interest

- Scientific data stewardship and long-term preservation
- Research repositories and open science
- Data governance, metadata, and quality
- The history of scientific data and the people who preserve it
